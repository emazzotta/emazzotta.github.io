### Visitor statistics

This website counts page views with Umami, which pleXius GmbH runs on its own server at Hetzner in Nuremberg (analytics.mazzotta.me). Requests to Umami pass through Cloudflare, Inc., USA, which protects the server against attacks. Umami sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. For each page view it stores the address and title of the page, the page you came from, your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language, and the country, region and city it derives from your IP address. pleXius GmbH logs each request to Umami with your IP address and usually keeps these logs for 30 days. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the page sends nothing to Umami.
